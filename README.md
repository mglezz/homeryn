# Homeryn

### Your home, in sync.

**One home. One shared space. Everything in its place.**

Keeping a home organized shouldn't be complicated. Whether you live with your family, your partner, or a group of roommates, there's always something to buy, something to do, or something someone forgot.

**Homeryn** is a simple app designed to make everyday life at home a little easier.

It brings shopping lists, household chores, and shared responsibilities together in one place, so everyone knows what needs to be done without having to ask.

No more scattered notes, forgotten groceries, or endless messages about whose turn it is to clean the kitchen.

Just a better way to stay organized, together.

---

## What is Homeryn?

Homeryn is a shared household organizer that helps people living together manage their everyday responsibilities.

The idea is simple: **everyone in the same home shares the same information, and everyone stays up to date.**

Imagine you're at the supermarket while someone at home realizes you've run out of milk. They add it to your shared shopping list, and it appears on your device.

Or imagine sharing an apartment with three friends. Instead of arguing about whose turn it is to take out the trash, everyone can check their assigned tasks.

That's what Homeryn is about.

## What can you do with Homeryn?

### 🛒 Shared shopping lists

Keep all your household shopping in one place.

- Create shopping lists that everyone at home can access.
- Add, edit, or remove products whenever you need to.
- Mark items as purchased while you're shopping.
- See changes made by other members without refreshing anything.
- Keep track of what's left to buy.

Whether you're planning the weekly groceries or just need someone to pick up a few things, everyone stays on the same page.

### 🧹 Household chores

Organize daily responsibilities without unnecessary confusion.

- Create tasks for things that need to be done around the house.
- Assign tasks to specific household members.
- See which tasks are still pending.
- Mark tasks as completed.
- Keep everyone informed about what's already been taken care of.

From cleaning the bathroom to taking out the trash, everyone can see what needs attention.

### 🏠 Your home, your people

Every household is different, so Homeryn is designed around the people who share it.

- Create your own household.
- Invite family members, partners, or roommates.
- Share shopping lists and tasks with everyone in your household.
- Keep your household information separate from other households.

You don't need to live in a big family to use Homeryn. Two people sharing an apartment are more than enough.

### 🔄 Everything stays in sync

You shouldn't have to wonder whether you're looking at an outdated shopping list.

When someone makes a change, Homeryn is designed to keep everyone connected to the same information.

Add a product, complete a task, or update a list, and other connected members will see the change.

And when you come back later, your household information will still be there.

## How will it work?

Getting started with Homeryn is intended to be straightforward:

1. **Create your home.** Set up a shared space for your household.
2. **Invite your people.** Bring your family, partner, or roommates together.
3. **Start organizing.** Add groceries, create tasks, and assign responsibilities.
4. **Stay in sync.** Everyone can see changes and keep track of what needs to be done.

That's it. No complicated setup or technical knowledge should be necessary for everyday use.

## Our approach

Homeryn is built around a few simple ideas.

**Keep it simple.** The app should be easy to understand from the moment you open it. You shouldn't need a tutorial just to add milk to a shopping list.

**Make it useful.** Every feature should solve an everyday problem. There's no reason to add complexity just for the sake of having more options.

**Keep everyone connected.** A shared household works better when everyone has access to the same up-to-date information.

**Respect people's time.** Organizing your home should take seconds, not become another chore.

**Start small, grow thoughtfully.** Homeryn doesn't need to do everything. It needs to do the important things well.

## What's planned?

Homeryn is currently in its planning stage. Development will begin with a small, practical version focused on the essentials.

### First version

The initial goal is to build a working application where multiple people can connect to the same household and manage:

- Shared shopping lists.
- Household chores and responsibilities.
- Household members.
- Real-time updates between connected devices.
- Saved household information that remains available between sessions.

The priority is to make these core features simple, reliable, and genuinely useful.

### Ideas for the future

Once the basics are working well, there are several possibilities worth exploring:

- **Recurring tasks:** Automatically schedule chores that need to be done regularly.
- **Reminders:** Get notified when an important task is coming up.
- **Shopping suggestions:** Quickly add products you buy frequently.
- **Multiple shopping lists:** Keep groceries, household supplies, and other purchases separate.
- **Shared expenses:** Keep track of common household purchases and costs.
- **More devices:** Make Homeryn accessible from phones and other platforms.

These are ideas, not confirmed features or release commitments. The direction of the project may evolve as it develops.
